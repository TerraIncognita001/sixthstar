Sixth Star

Sixth Star is a fan channel that posts short clips of GTA 6 moments from livestreams, with captions and a credit to the streamer in every clip.

"Sixth Star uploader" is a private tool the channel owner runs on their own computer. It uploads the channel's own finished clips to the channel's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.

Sixth Star is not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, YouTube or the streamers featured. Streamers who want a clip removed or credited differently can get in touch through the contact below.
